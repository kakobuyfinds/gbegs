What to check in warehouse photos

Save the exact listing and selected variant so you can compare the warehouse quality-control photos with your order.

Shoes and slides

Check the size label on both shoes, compare heel and sole alignment, and ask for an insole measurement if you need more certainty about fit.

Hoodies and T-shirts

Compare laid-flat chest width and body length with a garment you own. Check print placement, embroidery and the selected color.

Jackets

Check sleeve length, shoulder width, lining and closures. Ask for close-up photos of the zipper or snaps if their condition is hard to see.

Estimate your total in USD

Convert each quote to USD first. Include domestic shipping and service fees if they apply, and use your destination and packed parcel size for the international quote.

SuperBuy spreadsheet FAQ

How do I use this SuperBuy spreadsheet?

Filter the catalog and download the rows you want to compare. Find the item by name on CAFANS, open its detail page, and check the available shopping-agent options for that listing.

What should I save with each product?

Save the original listing URL, size, color, quoted item price, quote date and any measurements you need. Add the agent, warehouse photo status and estimated shipping as you confirm them.

How do I compare two international shipping quotes?

Use the same destination and packed parcel dimensions. Check how each line calculates billable weight, then compare the total quote, delivery estimate, tracking and included services.
